Agricultural-Rental Machinery

A web platform that connects farmers needing agricultural machinery with owners who want to rent out their equipment.

FIND MACHINERY (FOR FARMERS) LIST YOUR MACHINE (FOR OWNERS)

HOW IT WORKS?

🌾

For Farmers

  1. Create an Account – Register as a farmer.
  2. Search Machinery – Find the equipment you need.
  3. Check Details & Availability – View price, location, specifications, and available dates.
  4. Send Rental Request – Select your dates and request the machine.
  5. Get Confirmation – Wait for the owner to accept the request.
  6. Rent & Use – Collect/use the machinery for your farming work.
  7. Complete & Review – Complete the rental and provide a rating/review.

🚜

For Machine Owners

  1. Create an Account – Register as a machinery owner.
  2. List Your Machine – Add machine details, photos, location, and rental price.
  3. Set Availability – Mark the dates when your equipment is available.
  4. Receive Rental Requests – View requests from farmers.
  5. Accept or Reject – Choose whether to approve each request.
  6. Complete the Rental – Provide the machinery for the agreed period.
  7. Manage Your Listings – Update availability, prices, and machine details.
